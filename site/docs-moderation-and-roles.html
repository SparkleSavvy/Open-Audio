Moderation pipeline & roles

Statuses, who may do what, and how deletion stays deliberate.

Track statuses

StatusMeaning
pendingIn the review queue; visible only to its author (My uploads).
verifiedPublic — plays in the feed, searchable, downloadable.
suspendedTemporarily muted — card stays in the feed, playback and downloads disabled.
rejectedNot published; the author sees the reason and can resubmit.

Deletion is different from suspension: a deleted track is removed from the database entirely, together with its files.

Lifecycle

  1. User uploads — track becomes pending.
  2. Moderator previews it in /admin → Moderation.
  3. Verify publishes; reject attaches a reason; suspend mutes without removing.
  4. A rejected track can be resubmitted by its author and re-enters the queue.
  5. An admin may file a delete request with a reason instead of deleting.
  6. The owner approves or rejects delete requests in a dedicated tab.
  7. On every status change the uploader receives a notification.
